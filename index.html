Everything a download manager should be

Clean like IDM, powerful like nothing else on Linux.

🚀

Multi-connection speed

Files are split into parallel connections and written in place — no slow merge step. Watch every connection's progress block by block, with live speed, ETA and a global speed limiter.

🧲

Catches every browser download

Click a download in your browser and NDM's dialog opens instead — files, apps, documents, even extension-less links. Login-gated files work too: NDM uses your browser's own session, exactly like IDM.

🎬

Video from 1,700+ sites

A download pill sits right on the video player — YouTube, Facebook (even private posts), and 1,700+ more sites. Pick your quality up to 4K, or grab audio-only MP3.

Silent auto-setup

The 3.7 MB installer is all you download. The video engine fetches itself in the background on first launch, verified by SHA-256 — you never see a setup wizard.

🔄

Resume that actually works

Power cut? Crash? Restart? NDM restores your list and resumes byte-exactly — including downloads that needed a login session. Expired link? Refresh the address and continue.

Good to know

Requirements

  • Ubuntu, Zorin OS, Linux Mint or any Debian-based distro (x86-64; ARM64 supported for the video engine)
  • WebKitGTK 4.1 and GTK 3 — installed automatically as dependencies
  • ffmpeg — also handled automatically by the package
  • Internet on first launch for the one-time video-engine setup (~83 MB, background)

Windows?

A Windows portable build is in progress and will appear on the releases page when ready.

Is NDM really free?

Yes — free and open source, no ads, no accounts, no tracking. The app sends exactly one anonymous install ping so the developer can count installations; nothing else ever leaves your machine.

How does the browser catch work?

The NDM extension notices downloads and video pages in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Chromium and Firefox, and hands them to the app through a tiny native bridge — the same trick IDM uses. Your browser cookies are captured only for the link you clicked, stored locally, and deleted with the entry.

Can it resume a failed download?

Yes — if the server supports range requests (most do), NDM resumes from the exact byte, even after a restart, and even for login-gated files whose session it saved.

Why is the installer only 3.7 MB?

IDM-style design: the app itself is small and native (Rust). The video engine (yt-dlp + deno) is fetched once, in the background, and verified against pinned SHA-256 hashes before it can run.

How do updates work?

NDM checks the release feed on launch. A normal update is one click; if a release is marked mandatory, older versions pause new downloads until updated — so security fixes reach everyone.
